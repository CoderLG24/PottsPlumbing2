# Potts Plumbing Website Redesign

## Goal

Replace the existing Potts Plumbing website with a polished, responsive site that introduces Garrett Potts's business, explains its residential plumbing services in North Georgia, and makes it easy to call or email.

## Approved direction

Rebuild the site from the ground up. Treat the existing site as a source of potential business facts and assets only; do not reuse its page layouts, styles, or scripts. Keep a simple four-page structure: Home, Services, About, and Contact.

## Audience and primary actions

- North Georgia homeowners looking for a dependable local plumber.
- Visitors should quickly understand the business, its likely service scope, and how to contact Garrett.
- Primary actions are calling `(770) 685-3901` and emailing `gtpservices212@gmail.com`.

## Visual direction

- Present Potts Plumbing as a trusted, approachable North Georgia plumbing business with a simple, confident, human-designed feel.
- Use the supplied logo artwork and a restrained white, dark-blue, and light-blue palette. Increase body and service text sizes, create clear section breaks, and rely on layout, spacing, and typography rather than invented graphics.
- Use only the supplied logo images; do not create graphic treatments from Garrett's name or initials, and do not use stock or generated photos.
- Ensure contrast, keyboard access, visible focus, responsive navigation, and reduced-motion support.

## Page structure

- **Home:** Logo-led hero with “Trusted by North Georgia,” email-first inquiry action, expandable residential service details, transparent-pricing and best-in-class-service messaging, service-area context, and a short owner introduction. Do not include reviews.
- **Services:** Organized descriptions of common residential plumbing work, using service categories drawn from the existing content as candidates pending Garrett's confirmation.
- **About:** A short, grounded introduction to Garrett and Potts Plumbing, without unsupported claims about company history, staff, licenses, or guarantees.
- **Contact:** Email is the visually primary action; phone is available as a discreet alternative here. Include service-area context and no unconfigured inquiry backend.

## Content and trust requirements

- Confirmed contact details: `(770) 685-3901` and `gtpservices212@gmail.com`.
- The stated service region is North Georgia. Use this representative list: Talking Rock, Jasper, Ellijay, Nelson, Tate, Ball Ground, Dawsonville, Dahlonega, and Cumming. Confirm actual availability with Garrett before launch.
- Do not include reviews on this version of the website.
- Do not publish business hours. Treat prior-site claims about licensing, insurance, 24/7 emergency response, pricing, years of experience, technicians, and guaranteed service as unverified. Do not publish them as facts without confirmation.
- Present transparent pricing as an explicit service commitment: explain that pricing is discussed before work begins. Avoid unsupported “no surprise” guarantees.
- “Trusted by North Georgia” and “best-in-class service” are requested brand messages; do not add fabricated reviews, awards, ratings, or quantified proof.
- The old site includes candidate services such as drains, water heaters, leaks, toilets, fixtures, and pipe repair. Confirm the final service list before treating it as definitive.

## Implementation architecture

Use accessible, semantic HTML with shared responsive CSS and a small vanilla JavaScript file for navigation and lightweight interactions. Keep the site static and dependency-free. Use direct `tel:` and `mailto:` links; any inquiry form must clearly explain that it opens the visitor's email app unless a real form service is configured.

## Search visibility

- Give each page a concise, unique title and description that reflect its actual content, with North Georgia and relevant residential plumbing terms used naturally.
- Keep important content in crawlable HTML with a clear primary heading, descriptive internal links, mobile-friendly layouts, a working favicon, and fast-loading assets.
- Do not promise first place or a particular rank. Search position depends on Google's systems and verified local information.
- Add canonical URLs and an absolute-URL sitemap after the final domain is confirmed. Submit the sitemap and monitor indexing/performance in Google Search Console after launch.
- Add LocalBusiness/Plumber structured data only after Garrett confirms the actual business address or service-area setup, categories, and contact details. Keep markup consistent with visible page content; do not add review ratings without genuine, approved reviews.
- Create city/county landing pages only where Potts Plumbing genuinely serves those communities and each page can provide useful, distinct information. Avoid thin pages created only to target keywords.
- Complete and verify the Google Business Profile, keep details consistent across legitimate directories, and request real customer reviews after jobs. On-site SEO alone cannot ensure a top result.
